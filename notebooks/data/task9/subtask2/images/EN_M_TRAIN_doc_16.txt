Alaska Airlines and Horizon flights resume after briefly grounded

Alaska Airlines and Horizon flights resumed after the company asked the Federal Aviation Administration to issue a ground stop for all departures nationwide due to a computer issue Wednesday morning.

"We're working to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.
